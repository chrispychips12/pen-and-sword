# Product

register: brand

## Users
Men aged 20 to 26, early in corporate careers, who want to dress sharper for the office without paying designer prices. They value versatility, self-care and practicality, and mostly browse on their phones.

## Product purpose
Pre-launch brand site for Pen and Sword menswear. Make the brand memorable, show the versatility idea ("from desk to drinks"), and present Collection 01 as placeholders that real products can replace without a redesign.

## Brand
- Values: understated elegance, expressive, versatile, accessible.
- Colours: Forest Green #333D29 (primary), Feather White #FFFEF4 (secondary). Nothing else.
- Type: The Seasons (display; Bellefair stands in until licensed). A quiet sans for body text.
- Mark: a curved sword crossed with a quill, meaning strength and intellect.
- Voice: succinct, self-assured, aspirational, with understated wit. Relatable, never stiff.
- Peers: COS, Milkbar. Interaction reference: 33-mm.com (collection rhythm and product rails).

## Anti-references
Black-and-gold luxury clichés, tech dark mode, loud streetwear energy, identical card grids, motion for its own sake.
